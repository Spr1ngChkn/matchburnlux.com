How a job runs

  1. Intake. You tell us what it is and confirm you own it.
  2. Footage in. You share a link from your own storage, or hand us a drive. Originals are never altered.
  3. Selects & cut. We pick the moments and cut them.
  4. Review. Watermarked proofs. One round of notes is included.
  5. Delivery. Masters and captions on payment in full. You post on your own accounts; we never sign in to them.

The house rules

  • Your footage, your rights. You confirm you own or control everything in it, including the music and anyone on camera.
  • No minors on camera without a guardian's signed release.
  • No copyrighted music we can't clear. We'll mute it or swap it.
  • Confirmed in writing before we open a single file: the job, the rights, the price.
  • Half up front. A 50% deposit books the job and starts the clock; it's non-refundable once we open your files. The balance is due net fifteen after delivery, as on the fee schedule.
  • Footage stays offline on our machines and is deleted TBD days after delivery.
  • Files are yours on payment in full. We show work in our portfolio only with your written OK.
  • No promises we can't keep. We cut for the story, not for the algorithm: no guaranteed views, monetization or claim-free uploads.
