The Hallway

Matchburn LUX is a consulting firm: conceptual and creative development, by appointment. We wanted the website to work the way we do. No banner, no pitch. A dark hallway, a frosted-glass door, and whatever you choose to try.

Knock on the glass and someone answers. Drop a note through the mail slot. Try the handle and the door asks for an appointment. Somewhere in the building there is a word that walks you further down the hall, to a second door that belongs to Spr1ngChkn Enterprises: game, tech and code, with a radio playing and a TV glowing behind the glass.

The numbers, as of this file: six days from the first line to two working doors. 118 commits. 12 pages. About 5,400 lines of plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no frameworks, no trackers and no cookies. Every sound you hear on the doors is built in the browser.

It was made with Claude, from Anthropic, as a working partner. It took the ideas that sat in our heads for years and put them on the screen in days. We designed it, we decided every detail, and we tested it by hand.

The hallway is open. More doors are coming.
