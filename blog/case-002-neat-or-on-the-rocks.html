Case File 002

“Neat, or on the rocks?”

Ice clinks. Liquid splashes. A sip. Needle drops, scratches, catches the groove — the record settles, and the music begins to fill the room, pouring into the hallway faster than the smoke, which follows the fragrant aroma of a freshly lit cigar. Ah — this explains the spark: so stark, so incognito, behind frosted glass, voices enveloped in ambience.

What gets discussed in there?

Not the pitch. The thing underneath it, the flaw nobody inside still has the distance left to see.

By appointment only. Not every case gets taken. The ones that do get finished.
